What life would hold, what I would come to see.
I looked around and wondered if I'd find
A path that felt like it was truly mine.

That I am the one who answers to the call.
No one can walk this journey in my stead —
The pen is in my hand, the choice is mine to read.

✨ Short Story: The Start That Found Its Way

Lila loved stargazing. Every night she'd sit by her window and watch the sky, wondering what it would be like to reach one of those little twinkling lights. But she always felt small — like she was just one person on a big planet with no special purpose at all.


One cloudy night, the sky went dark. No stars shone through. Lila felt lonely. She lit a candle and set it on her windowsill just to have a little light of her own.


As she sat there, a soft glow caught her eye — not from above, but from the garden below. There, a single firefly danced in the shadows, bright and steady even though the moon was hidden away.


Lila smiled. "You don't need the stars to shine," she whispered to herself.


From that night on she realized something beautiful: You don't have to be big or famous to make a difference. Sometimes all it takes is one small light burning bright right where you stand. And that was enough. 💛
